Emotional and Wellness Data
The Application collects emotional data that you voluntarily provide through the emotion tracking features, including:

Emotional states and feelings you select during journaling sessions (e.g., mood levels, specific emotions)
Emotional exploration paths and patterns recorded through the app's 5-level emotion tree
Optional notes or memos you attach to your records
Frequency and timing of your journaling activity

This data is classified as personal health and wellness data. It is stored locally on your device and is not transmitted to external servers or shared with third parties, except as described in this policy. This data is used solely to provide the core journaling and statistics features of the Application, and is never used for advertising purposes.
You may delete your emotional records at any time through the app. Uninstalling the Application will remove all locally stored data from your device.
